## Scope and limitation

This is **a proposed structured educational map, not actual course content and not proof of efficacy or clinical training accreditation**. No lessons, books, references, copyright grants, patient consent, qualified instructors or credentials are invented. File05 must not auto-publish or import this blueprint. Every real lesson needs approved source text, author, edition, page, rights, review and explicit next action. Some topics describe historical homeopathic claims; scientific claims must carry appropriate evidence grading, limitations and medical-safety review.
